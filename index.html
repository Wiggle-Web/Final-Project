The Cosmere

Welcome to this fanmade cosmere fansite where you can find interesting facts about different characters in the worlds. Just click on the world you want and it will direct you to a page with information about many of the different characters

Roshar

This planet is well known for its ferocious storms.
The books that are set on this planet include Stormlight archive, Edge Dancer, and Dawnshard.

Scadrial

Scadrial is a planet thats known by its red sun and lack of a moon.
The books that are set on this planet are mistborn era one and mistborn era 2

Nalthis

This planet is known by its capitol city T'Telir with the court of the gods.
The books that are set in this planet is Warbreaker
