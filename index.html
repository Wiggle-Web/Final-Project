The Cosmere

Welcome to this fanmade cosmere fansite where you can find interesting facts about different characters in the worlds

Roshar

This planet is well known for its ferocious storms and the books that are set on this planet include Stormlight archive, Edge Dancer, and Dawnshard.

Scadrial

Scadrial is a planet thats known by its red sun and lack of a moon the books that are set on this planet are mistborn era oen and mistborn era 2

Sel

This planet is known by its legendary city Elantris the books that take place on this planet are Elantris and The Emporers Soul
